FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Which countries can I study in?

Available destinations and programmes depend on your academic profile, goals, budget and current university offerings.

Can you help me choose a course?

Yes. Course selection can consider your academic background, career goals, preferred destination and budget.

Do you provide visa guidance?

Yes. Guidance can cover documentation and the application process. Final visa decisions are made by the relevant authorities.

Do I need IELTS?

Requirements vary by country, university and programme. Some institutions may accept alternatives.
